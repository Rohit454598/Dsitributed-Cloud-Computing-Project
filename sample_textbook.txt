Indus Valley Civilization

The Indus Valley Civilization (c. 3300–1300 BCE) was one of the world's earliest urban civilizations.
It developed along the Indus River and nearby regions in present-day Pakistan and northwest India.

Urban Planning
Cities such as Mohenjo-Daro and Harappa had carefully planned grid layouts, baked-brick houses,
advanced drainage systems, and public buildings. Streets were often oriented in a grid pattern.

Trade and Economy
The civilization engaged in extensive trade with Mesopotamia and used standardized weights and measures.
Crafts such as bead making, pottery, and metallurgy were important economic activities.

Writing System
A script, still undeciphered, was used for communication and record-keeping. Seals with animal motifs
and inscriptions are among the most famous artifacts.

Social Structure
Evidence suggests a well-organized society. There is little clear evidence of a strong centralized monarchy.
Public works and uniform planning point to organized civic administration.

Decline
The civilization declined due to factors such as climate change, river shifts, or possible invasions.
Despite its decline, it remains significant for its contributions to early urban development.

Neural Networks

A neural network is a computational model inspired by the structure and function of the human brain.
It consists of layers of interconnected nodes called neurons.

Layers
- Input Layer: Receives raw data for processing.
- Hidden Layers: Perform computations, extract patterns, and process information using weights, biases,
  and activation functions.
- Output Layer: Produces the final result based on the learned patterns.

Learning
Each connection between neurons has a weight, which determines the strength of the signal, and a bias,
which fine-tunes the output. Neural networks learn through training using algorithms such as
backpropagation and optimization methods like gradient descent.

Applications
Neural networks are used in deep learning and power applications in image recognition, natural language
processing, speech recognition, and recommendation systems.
